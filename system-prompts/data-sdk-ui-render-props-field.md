@internal The component's plain-data props as the asking surface holds them; the `ui.render` site rule validates their shape per component. `output` on a ToolUse is capped at 64 KB before the chain sees it. An `AgentRow` ask names its agent by `instance_id` and may say `columns`, its row's width: the engine fills the rest from the task it holds, answers its own node and `{}` for an agent it does not hold, and says in the answer's `props.isHidden` whether the row leaves the surface's at-a-glance list. The answer's `props.label` is there only when a hook changed it. A `Theme` ask under `backdrop` may say `held`, at most 8 lowercase SHA-256 hashes of what the client already holds (an Svg's `source` as UTF-8, a Shader's `ir` as RFC 8785 JSON); no hook sees it, and that answer alone may name those parts by hash. A `ContextMenu` ask says what the person opened the menu on, each part optional: `row` ({component, requestId}: the transcript row, named as in that row's own ui_render ask), `link` ({href, text?}), `selection` ({text, requestId?}) and `code` ({text, isBlock}); a part of another shape, empty, or too long (text over 64 KB, an `href` over 2,048 characters, an id over 256) is dropped. The answer's `props` are empty. Use one `instance_id` per window. A new menu asked under it can release the buttons of the menu before it, so press only buttons from the newest menu. The engine asks each plugin by itself and answers their trees stacked in one column, or its own node when none drew: the surface makes a row of each Button and each Link, leaves out the rest, and reads neither `props` nor `rewritten`.

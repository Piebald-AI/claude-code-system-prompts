@internal What to draw, or `Theme`, the session's theme, by `instance_id`. Under `theme` and `image:<id>:<n>` no hook draws: the engine answers itself, `props` of `plugin` with whichever of `colors`, `overrideColorScheme` and a still `backdrop` stands, or `data`, `index`, `of`, `hash` and `bytes` for a piece of an image, to `desktop` and `mobile`, and `{}` with none standing, with a scene and no `colors`, under another id or to any other surface. To `desktop` alone, `theme` also carries `plugin` and `soundHash` wherever the theme has a sound, and `sound:<soundHash>` answers `sound` for any of the last 8 hashes that a `theme` answer gave. A style travels the same way, as `styleHash`, and `style:<styleHash>` answers `style`. Under `backdrop` a mod's `ui.render` hook for `Backdrop` draws the scene that stands, with `props` of `scene`, `plugin`, `glass` and `overrideColorScheme` where a plugin said it.

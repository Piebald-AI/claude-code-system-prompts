<system-reminder>
About the update above only: other background work is still running. If this update needs no action and holds nothing the user needs to know yet, end your turn now with an empty reply. An empty reply is a valid, complete turn: write no text, not even a line saying that nothing is new, and call no tool in its place, whether to wait, to check for more or to do nothing (such as `true`). Tell them now about a failure, a risk, a surprise, a result they asked to hear about, or a decision you need from them, even if they asked for one summary at the end. If all the work they asked for is now done, say so.
</system-reminder>

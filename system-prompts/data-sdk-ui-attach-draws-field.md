@internal What this client draws beyond what every client of its surface draws: `Theme` (what `$.theme.set` sets, the scene behind the conversation and the look alike; only a `desktop` or `mobile` client's word counts), `Input` and `Select` (on `mobile`: a client that does not name one is sent an empty Box in its place, as is an ask with no `client_id`, since no attach can name its default id), `Progress` (on `mobile`: a client that does not name it, or an ask with no `client_id`, is sent the Box and Text that draw it). Held per client like `answers`: a later `ui_attach` under the same id that names it replaces it, and it leaves with the client. A name this version does not know, or one said twice, is ignored, so a newer client keeps its attach; a list of more than 16 names, or a name of more than 64 characters, is refused with the attach. Absent: a client still attached, or one put back, keeps what it said, and a first attach draws none. Empty, or nothing known in it: none, and `$.theme.set` answers that nothing draws it.
